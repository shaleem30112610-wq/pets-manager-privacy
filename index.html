Privacy Policy for Pets Manager

Developer: SBTECHLABS
Effective date: October 13, 2026
Contact: shaleem30112610@gmail.com

SBTECHLABS respects your privacy. This policy explains how Pets Manager (the “App”) handles information when you use it.

1. Information you enter

The App allows you to enter and manage information about pets and kennel activities, which may include pet names, photos, species, breeds, dates, health and vaccination records, medication schedules, breeding and litter records, expenses, income, sales, and related notes. Depending on what you choose to record, entries may also include contact details or other information about people involved in pet-related transactions.

2. Storage and use of data

According to the App's intended configuration, records are stored locally on your device and are used to provide the App's pet management, recordkeeping, and reminder features. The App does not require registration or login. SBTECHLABS does not operate a user-account database for the App and does not receive the records you enter through the App's core local-storage functionality.

3. Sharing and third-party services

SBTECHLABS does not sell your pet records or use them for advertising. The App's core functionality is not designed to upload or share your locally stored records with SBTECHLABS or third parties. If you use operating-system features such as device backups, the platform provider may handle backup data under its own terms and privacy policy. Any third-party SDKs or services included in a future version will be disclosed in an updated policy as appropriate.

4. Permissions

Depending on the features you use and your Android version, the App may request permission to send notifications or access photos or files you choose to add. Such access is intended only to support relevant App features. You can manage permissions in Android settings.

5. Data security

Locally stored data is subject to the protections provided by your device and Android operating system. We recommend using a screen lock and keeping your device updated. No method of data storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete it using available App functions, clear the App's storage through Android settings, or uninstall the App, subject to any operating-system backups. Clearing App storage or uninstalling the App generally removes its local data; data retained in a device backup may need to be removed separately using your backup provider's controls. Because no account is created, there is no App account to delete. You may contact us for privacy questions, but we generally cannot access or remotely delete information stored only on your device.

7. Children's privacy

Pets Manager is intended as a pet-management utility and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the App's core local-storage functionality. If you have a concern, contact us at the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or its data practices change. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact us

For privacy questions, contact SBTECHLABS at shaleem30112610@gmail.com.
